The Kingdom DEIB office stands for a continual progression of increasing diversity, equity, inclusion and belonging, and as an advocate for the diversification and inclusion policies of the kingdom, as well as for individual members within the kingdom who need a voice in these matters.

This office is currently held by Lady Adelheid Krämer Von Rotemburg, who herself is blind, autistic, suffering from scoliosis and sciatica.

The Kingdom DEIB Officer is supported by shire officers and a newly created principality officer in the Principality of Insulae Draconis. Together they intend to create a positive and accessible environment, working with event staff and offices both at the highest levels of the kingdom and within each principality and barony, to broaden the horizon of DEIB going forward.

If members old and new have any queries or concerns they wish to raise, they are welcome to email the DEIB Officer at deib@drachenwald.sca.org.

## Current Work and Initiatives
* Creating new DEIB offices across the kingdom
* Working toward a seated fencing tournament
* An annual blind fencing event
* Classes on DEIB topics
* Initiatives to improve the mental health of members, such as providing quiet rooms at events
* Liaising with the marshallate disciplines and with the chatelaines of the kingdom, to put diversity, equity and inclusion at the heart of welcoming new members and to increase a feeling of belonging

The office also liaises quarterly with the Society DEIB Officer, as well as with the officers of the kingdom.

## Former DEI Council
The DEIB Officer replaces the Diversity, Equity, and Inclusion (DEI) Council, which has been disbanded. Information about the former council is kept on the [DEI Council]({{ site.baseurl}}{% link offices/seneschal/diversity-equity-inclusion-council.md %}) page for historical reference.
