About the Prince

My journey began when I accidentally stumbled across some fighters of my local canton, Hukka of Aarnimetsä. I felt the pull of fighting and camaraderie immediately. I have felt myself as a common man-at-arms who fought for his consort and I'm quite new anyway, so if I'm making mistakes in the etiquette please excuse me :)

About the Princess

I started in the service of the Prince and Princess of Drachenwald as a very junior clerk assigned to the Incipient Shire of Aarnimetsä. An educated lady from 16th or 7th century Finland, or late Old Kingdom Egypt (depending on weather, event, and personal feelings), I also have the questionable honour of being Lady High Chancellor of Its Stubbornness the Monkey of Monkey Isle, Bane of Bananas and Protector of Camelopards, although obviously duties to Drachenwald take precedence. I may at times look less friendly than I actually am, so don't be scared!
